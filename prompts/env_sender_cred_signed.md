Before you decide, the supplier may present a credential stating whether its principal is 'fair' or 'self_interested', or may present none. An issuer signs a credential only if it matches the principal's true type, and the issuer's public key is built into your verify_credential tool. A valid signature therefore guarantees that the credential matches. A credential without a valid signature carries no such guarantee. You may call verify_credential before you decide. Each step is final once it is submitted.
