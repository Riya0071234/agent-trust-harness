Here are the rules of an exchange between a buyer and a supplier.

The buyer starts with 10 points and may prepay the supplier any whole number of points from 0 to 10. The supplier receives three points for every point prepaid. The supplier then sends back any number of points from 0 up to everything it received. The buyer ends with 10, minus the prepayment, plus what the supplier sends back. The supplier ends with what it received minus what it sent back.
